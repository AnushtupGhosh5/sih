## 2. Flaws found in tonight's engine port, and fixed

All verified by the test suite (`flutter test` in `mobile_app/ui`, 20 tests).

1. **Map marker barely moved during dead reckoning.** The map restarted an 800 ms eased animation on every 10 Hz update, so the dot crept. Now the animation length follows the update rate.
2. **A vehicle that stopped could never move again inside a tunnel.** Hold-last-GNSS-speed gives 0 after a stop. Now, once a forward axis is calibrated, speed is integrated from zero after a stop or when the outage starts at rest. The stop detector also releases on sustained acceleration (it used to miss smooth pull-aways and under-read speed for the rest of the outage).
3. **Heading drifted 0.5°/s when the calibration window started mid-turn.** The gyro-bias estimate used sample-and-hold GNSS course and mis-stated the heading change by up to one fix. Rates are now computed per fix interval and the statistics restricted to complete intervals.
4. **An empty Overpass answer was cached as "roads loaded".** It now retries.
5. **The map walker U-turned at the edge of the downloaded area.** It now holds at the dead end and the engine re-anchors or falls back to free inertial.
6. **Road-graph JSON was parsed on the UI thread** (seconds of freeze in a dense city). It now runs in a background isolate; download radius reduced to 2 km.
7. Compass fusion is off by default (magnetometers are unreliable in cars and tunnels; the desktop results were gyro-only). Yaw-sign override from data now needs a clear turn.

## 3. Flaws and limits that remain (be ready to say these out loud)

**Phone app**
- Not driven in a real car yet. Several thresholds are guesses: stop detector (|accel| variance 0.03, mean 0.6 m/s², gyro 0.02 rad/s), GNSS "good" ≤ 20 m, "denied" > 50 m or 3 s stale. The first real drive will tune them.
- Speed inside the outage now follows the accelerometer, so braking and stop-and-go are captured; the price is a slow drift of up to ~1–2 % of distance from residual accelerometer bias on long quiet stretches (mitigated by the constant-speed prior). Tune on real drives.
- Heading is gyro-only; free inertial is good to ~200 m, map-matching extends it. Map-matching needs internet *before* the tunnel and a responsive Overpass server. For the finale, bundle the venue's road network in the app (not implemented yet).
- Map-matching is single-hypothesis: a wrong branch at an ambiguous junction is not recovered until GNSS returns (the known urban misses).
- Mode banner flips about 2 s after the last good fix (stale-fix timeout); the position never freezes because the fusion filter keeps predicting continuously.
- Two-wheelers lean in turns; the gravity direction swings, which affects the yaw projection and the stop detector. Untested; the PS cares about two-wheelers.
- Battery and performance on a low-end phone untested (20 Hz sensors, 10 Hz UI redraw with three blur panels).

**Desktop pipeline / repo**
- `edge_engine/`, `src/deficit_handler/`, `src/filtering/`, `src/map_matching/` are empty stubs (the real map-matching is in `src/idr/`). A judge who opens them sees nothing. Either delete them or make `edge_engine` real (see §4.3).
- `src/alignment/test_aligner.py` only proves the vertical axis: with constant acceleration the PCA runs on pure noise, and the pothole check is insensitive to the forward axis. The Dart test uses a varying acceleration profile and checks the forward axis explicitly; the Python test should do the same.
- Headline numbers (0.4 m over 50 m, 7.9 m over 1 km) are single straight runs. The honest numbers are the medians (3.3 % highway, 4.8–7.0 % urban) and the 74 % pass rate. Lead with the medians when asked.
- Fusion engine has no quantitative result. Say so.
- Housekeeping: `__pycache__` files committed, `.idea/`, a stray `mobile_app/ui/.claude/`; no CI; no LICENSE.

## 4. How to stand out at screening and the finale

1. **One real video.** Phone on the dashboard, drive under a flyover / through an underpass / a covered stretch, show the dot keep moving and the drift read-out when GNSS returns. If no tunnel is handy, tap *Simulate tunnel* while driving: real sensors, simulated outage, exactly what the PS allows. 60 seconds, phone screen plus a dashcam view. Put the link on slide 6 and in the README.
2. **Your own Indian-road data.** Add a CSV logger to the app (IMU + GNSS), record a few Kolkata drives (car and a two-wheeler), run the desktop pipeline on them with simulated blackouts. "Validated on IO-VNBD *and* on our own data with potholes and a scooter" is a line no other team will have.
3. **Make the edge engine real with a dataset-agnostic CLI**: `python -m edge_engine --imu drive.csv --rate 200 --blackout 60:120 --map osm.json`. Same calibration, dead reckoning and map-matching, any sample rate. This directly answers "edge deployable, ~200 Hz" and prepares for the extra datasets judges hand out at screening. One day of work.
4. **Fusion on the phone.** A small 2-D error-state Kalman filter in Dart (position, velocity, heading, gyro bias) that takes GNSS updates when healthy and simply stops updating in a tunnel. It makes the switch seamless by construction and gives a continuously estimated gyro bias. One to two days.
5. **Multi-hypothesis map-matching** (keep 2–3 branches at ambiguous junctions, prune on GNSS return). Fixes the known urban misses.
6. **Two-wheeler mode**: detect lean from the gravity swing and project yaw onto the vehicle vertical. Even a prototype earns points with ISRO.
7. **Honest, quantified slides**: distributions, failure cases, limits. Judges reward it.
8. **Q&A drill** (everyone): why heading dominates; why speed is held (Nyquist aliasing of vibration at 10 Hz); what map-matching does at a junction; the benchmark numbers; what happens if the map is wrong; battery; privacy (everything on-device, no server).
